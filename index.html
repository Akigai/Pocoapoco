La idea: 8 semanas

A two-month sprint, about 90 minutes a day. Sounds like a lot, but half of it is watching and listening to things you love. And you have a huge head start: four years in Barcelona. The Spanish is already inside you; these 8 weeks aren't about putting it in, they're about getting it out.

The rules: miss a day and nothing happens, the plan continues tomorrow. If a week feels too big, repeat it; the order matters more than the speed. And mistakes aren't failures, they're the training material. Every corrected mistake is a sentence that's now yours.

El día (about 90 min)

  • Mañana: journal, one paragraph (5 min). With your coffee.
  • Palabras: 10 new ones, logged here (10 min). From what you watch and hear.
  • Hablar: AI conversation out loud (20 min). The prompt lives in the Hoy tab.
  • Ver o escuchar: something you love, in Spanish (35 min).
  • Eco: repeat sentences out loud, copying the audio (10 min).
  • Noche: journal, one paragraph (5 min). Before bed.

La semana

  • Lunes a sábado: the normal day above.
  • Domingo, light day: review the week's words with the Repasar button, watch a long episode, that's it. Skip the journal if you don't feel like it.
  • One small mission a week (in Barcelona): order patatas bravas on a terraza in Spanish and add one extra question, ask something at a La Boqueria stall, tell the barista what you thought of the Barça game. In Germany: a video call in Spanish, or a Spanish bar or café.

La ruta: 8 semanas

Each week has a colour and a focus. Open a week, tick its tasks, and finish the Sunday round-up. Tap "Estoy aquí" to mark the week you're on.

La Liga de Alisha

Eight weeks, eight fixtures. A strong week (5+ days played, 3+ perfect) wins the match. Week 8 is El Clásico: the B1 final.

La vitrina

Trophies are earned, never given. Grey means still waiting.

La misión de la semana

One real conversation in the wild each week. Claude gives you a concrete mission with the exact phrases; afterwards, tell it how it went and get a debrief.

El Repaso · the weekly round-up

On Sunday (or whenever): Claude reads your week's journals, corrections and words and gives you a report: what improved, the stubborn mistakes, a mini-quiz, and an honest verdict: move on or repeat.

¿Qué tan lejos está B1?

Claude reads your recent writing and gives an honest estimate. Check once a week and watch it climb.

Copia de seguridad

Your journal, words and progress live on this phone only. Download a backup once a week; restore it if you ever switch phones or clear Safari data.

Menú de inmersión

Choose for pleasure, not duty. If it bores you, change it.

  • Football (the best route!): Barça's Spanish content: post-match interviews with Pedri, Aitana and Alexia (very clear Spanish), Messi interviews, El Chiringuito (glorious chaos), Mundo Deportivo and Sport.es headlines. For Sports Management: read Barça's official statements and club business news in Spanish; that's where your industry's vocabulary lives.
  • Food: Paulina Cocina (huge cooking channel on YouTube), Spanish food vlogs; always read menus in Spanish. You live in the city of bravas; use it!
  • F1 and tennis: DAZN F1 España, SoyMotor (YouTube), Alonso and Sainz interviews in Spanish; in tennis, Alcaraz and Nadal press conferences.
  • Shopping and travel: Spanish fashion and haul vlogs, Vogue España; for travel: Molaviajar, Enrique Alex, Luisito Comunica.
  • Series: a series you already know, dubbed into Spanish (brilliant trick for starting), or Valeria, Machos Alfa, Élite. Spanish audio + Spanish subtitles.
  • YouTube: Dreaming Spanish (made for learning by listening), Easy Spanish (street interviews), Español con Juan, plus cooking, fashion and daily-life vloggers in Spanish.
  • Podcasts: Duolingo Spanish Podcast (easy stories), Hoy Hablamos, Español con Juan. Later on: Nadie Sabe Nada.
  • Reading: 20minutos or RTVE headlines with your coffee. Just the headlines at first.
  • Your phone in Spanish. Instagram, Netflix, the whole phone. Free immersion all day long.

Para la confianza

Four years in Barcelona means there's already a lot of Spanish inside you; more than you think. What's missing isn't talent, it's speaking mileage. That's why the AI conversation is sacred: it's speaking with no audience, no embarrassment and no rush. Within a few weeks, talking to real people will just be a little more of the same.

And one more thing: nobody in Spain speaks perfect Spanish either. Say things wrong with confidence. It works.
